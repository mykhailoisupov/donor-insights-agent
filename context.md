# Context notes

Background the agent can use to explain causes. Numbers still come from the data.

## Organisation

Brightpath Education Fund is a nonprofit that funds scholarships for students in Ukraine.
Donors are individuals (mostly US and Ukraine) and a small group of foundations.
The fiscal year runs January to December.

## How donations are recorded

- One-off gifts are made online by card or PayPal.
- Recurring gifts are monthly card or PayPal subscriptions, charged on the day the donor signed up.
- Foundations give by wire, usually quarterly or yearly, so their months are uneven.
- Every December gift is tagged with the `year_end` campaign.

## Known events

- 2024-11: Northbridge Family Foundation made a one-time $5,000,000 gift for a new scholarship endowment. It will not repeat.
- 2025-03-03 to 2025-03-05: our card processor had an outage. Failed renewals cancelled many card subscriptions. PayPal was not affected.
- 2025-12: first year-end campaign with paid social ads and a matching gift from the board.
- 2026-02: new donation page launched with PayPal as the default payment option.
- 2026-05: Harbor Light Trust, which gave about $40,000 a month, made no gift. Our team has not heard from them.
